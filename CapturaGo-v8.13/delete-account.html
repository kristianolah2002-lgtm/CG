Delete your account

CapturaGo website and the CapturaGo app for iPhone and Android

You can delete your CapturaGo account at any time — here, or in the app under Account → Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password)
  • Your creator listing, its photos, packages and local spots
  • Inquiries sent to your listing, your favourites, and the reviews you wrote
  • Your private conversations (they disappear for the other person too)

What we keep

  • Records of paid bookings (amounts, dates, names) — accounting and tax law requires us to keep them, in Slovakia generally for 10 years. They are no longer linked to an account.
  • Inquiries you sent with the contact form (by email) stay with the creators who received them.

If you have an active booking (paid, confirmed, delivered or under review), finish or cancel it first.

Checking whether you're signed in…

Can't sign in any more? Email hello@capturago.com from the address you registered with and we'll delete it within 30 days.
