3. What is public

If you create a creator listing, everything in the listing except your contact email is visible to anyone visiting CapturaGo. A creator's intro video is shown as a link only; it opens on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or Vimeo, which then process your data under their own privacy policies — nothing is loaded from them until you tap it. Reviews are public together with the reviewer's display name and any photos the reviewer added to the review. Private messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. Inquiries are private — only the creator you message can see them.

3a. Private messages

Messages between a traveller and a creator can be read only by the two of them. They are encrypted while they travel (HTTPS) and while stored (our database provider encrypts its disks). CapturaGo staff do not read your messages. The only exception: if one of you reports a conversation, our team can read that conversation to investigate scams, abuse or a booking dispute. You can block a conversation at any time; then neither side can send new messages. If a message stays unread, we send you a short email that someone wrote to you — without the message itself. If a traveller has waited 2 hours for a creator's answer, we remind the creator once by email (again without the message).

When you tap Translate under a message, the text of that one message is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to translate it into your language. The translation is stored with the message and deleted together with it. Messages are never sent anywhere unless you tap Translate.

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. We chose this so that you never lose your conversations when you reset your password or change phones, and so that we can help when a conversation is reported.

3b. Delivered photos

Photos a creator uploads for your booking are stored in a private storage area. Only you and that creator can see them, through temporary links that expire after a few hours; they are never public and never shown on profiles. The traveller can mark favourites; the creator can see which photos were marked. We delete the photos 90 days after the booking is completed (and 30 days after a booking is cancelled or refunded), and we email the traveller a reminder about 2 weeks before. Please download the photos you want to keep. Photos are also deleted when the traveller deletes their account.

Sharing with friends: the traveller can create a view-only link to the gallery (valid 1–30 days, optionally only the favourites). Anyone who has the link can view the photos, the shoot date and the creator's name until the link expires or the traveller turns it off — never the traveller's name, email or payment details. Expired links are deleted 7 days after they expire.

Portfolio photos that a creator adds to their listing are public, like the rest of the listing.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and listing data: until you delete your listing or ask us to delete your account.
  • Inquiries: until the creator's listing is deleted, or earlier if you ask us to delete them.
  • Private messages: until either person in the conversation deletes their account (then the whole conversation is deleted), or earlier if you ask us. Reports are kept while the reported conversation exists.
  • Booking and payment records: as long as accounting and tax law requires (in Slovakia generally 10 years), even if a listing or account is deleted.
  • Delivered photos: 90 days after the booking is completed, 30 days after it is cancelled, declined or refunded, or when the traveller deletes their account — whichever comes first.
  • Message translations: together with the message they belong to.
  • Search insights: 180 days, then deleted automatically.
  • City alerts: until you remove them (dashboard or app → Account) or delete your account.
  • "Notify me" about a creator: until you remove it or delete your account; at most 60 days after we told you, or 67 days after you asked if nothing opened up.
  • Founding creator spots: while your account exists (needed to keep the one-spot-per-person rule); deleted with your account.
  • Quick replies: until you delete them or your account. Email preferences and your language: while your account exists.
  • Mobile number for text reminders: until you remove it or delete your account; a number that was never confirmed is deleted after 2 days. Code send times: 2 days.
  • Last-minute deals: 14 days after the day of the deal. The record of which day you got a deal email: 7 days.
  • Calendar sync: the calendar link until you disconnect it or delete your account; the busy dates are replaced every time we read your calendar and removed when you disconnect. Calendar feed link: until you make a new one or delete your account.
  • Trips: until you remove them; trips in the past are dropped when you next save your trips.
  • Reply time: recalculated every hour from the last 90 days; nothing older is used.
  • Shared gallery links: until you turn them off, and at most 7 days after they expire.
  • Reports of profiles or photos: until the reported listing is deleted; the link to your account is removed when you delete it.
  • Shoot plan and problem reports: with the booking record.
  • Reviews: while the reviewed listing exists, unless you ask us to remove your review.
  • Gift cards: as long as accounting law requires for the payment (generally 10 years); failed code checks: 1 hour for the limit, then deleted with routine housekeeping.
  • Split payment shares and partner thank-you records: with the booking record.
  • Shoot requests and offers: while your account exists; requests are closed automatically when their dates have passed. We may delete closed requests after 12 months.
  • Invite codes and rewards: while the accounts exist. Partner applications: while the partnership exists, and rejected applications for up to 12 months.
  • Server logs: according to our hosting providers' standard retention (usually days to weeks).

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data (creators can edit their listing anytime in the dashboard);
  • have your data deleted (creators can delete their listing in the dashboard; for full account deletion email us);
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time (e.g. location access in your browser settings);
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work.

Deleting your account: you can delete it yourself at any time on capturago.com/delete-account.html or in the CapturaGo app (Account → Delete account). This removes your login, listing, photos, favourites and reviews; booking records are kept as accounting law requires.

To use any of these rights, email hello@capturago.com.

8. Age

CapturaGo is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on encrypted disks. Access to data in our database is restricted by row-level security rules so that users can only change their own data. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced on the website.
