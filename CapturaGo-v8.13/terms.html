That copy is what both sides agreed and is used if there's a problem, even if the creator changes the package later. If the photos are promised "within N days", that time counts from the shoot date and we remind the creator when it's due.
  • Prices and fees. Creators set the price of each package. Clients pay the package price plus a CapturaGo service fee (currently 10%, or 5% when the client books through the creator's personal CapturaGo link), shown before payment. Creators currently receive 100% of their package price. We'll give at least 30 days' notice before changing creator fees.

  • Payment held until the photos arrive. If the package promises the photos within a set number of days, the payment stays on hold until the creator has sent the photos through CapturaGo, and is released 3 days after that (and never earlier than 3 days after the shoot) — unless the client releases it sooner or reports a problem. Once the promised date has passed without the photos, the client can report the photos as late; the payment stays frozen while we review it.
  • Creators on a trip. Creators can list trips ("in Lisbon 3–10 Nov"). A booking on a trip day takes place in the trip city, which is shown before payment and in the booking. The creator must be in that city on the booked day; a trip can't be removed or moved away from a booked shoot. Creators are responsible for being allowed to work where they travel (for example visas, permits and taxes).

  • Days and times. Creators can mark days they are not available and how many shoots they take per day; those days can't be booked. The client can choose a preferred time of day; the creator shares the exact meeting point and time in the booking (and the client gets an email with a map link).

The booking only changes if the other side accepts it, and moving is free — up to 3 times per booking. If heavy rain is forecast for the shoot day, our reminder email the day before and the text reminder on the day suggest moving it, with a link to propose a new date; nothing changes unless the other side accepts. Bad weather alone isn't a reason for a refund. If you can't agree on a new date and the creator can't do the shoot, the creator cancels and the client is refunded in full; otherwise the normal cancellation rules below apply. The weather forecast we show comes from a third-party service and may be wrong.
  • Shoot plan and surprises. The client can add a shot list and reference links, and can mark a shoot as a surprise (for example a proposal). For a surprise, the creator must keep it secret and must not contact or tag the other person before the moment, and our emails to the client use neutral subjects. We can't control other ways the surprise might be discovered (such as shared devices or bank statements).
  • Cancellation by the client. Full refund (including the service fee) if the creator hasn't confirmed yet, or if a confirmed booking is cancelled at least 7 days before the shoot date. Later cancellations are not automatically refunded; either side can propose a new date instead (see "Moving a shoot").

  • Remote work (editing, clipping, retouching, thumbnails, UGC). The package shows the delivery time and number of revisions. The client can cancel for a full refund until the creator accepts the order, or if the work isn't delivered by the due date. After delivery, the client approves the work or requests an included revision. The payment is released when the client approves, or 3 days after delivery if the client doesn't respond or report a problem. Clients must only share files they have the right to share. The payout is then frozen while we review the case with both sides and decide on a full, partial or no refund. This doesn't affect your statutory rights.

6d. Drone pilots

Creators who offer drone services must give the details required where they fly, and confirm that they follow the local rules:

  • EU, EEA and Switzerland (EASA rules): registration as a drone operator — required for any drone with a camera, whatever its weight, for paid and hobby flights alike — and, for drones of 250 g or more, the A1/A3 or A2 certificate of competency.
  • United States (FAA rules): for paid work, an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate and a Part 107 drone registration, regardless of the drone's weight.
  • Other countries: any permit or registration the local authority requires.

These numbers are stored privately and are not shown on the creator's profile; the profile only shows that they were declared. CapturaGo does not verify them. The creator alone is responsible for flying legally and safely, including no-fly zones, Remote ID and any insurance required.

6b. How creators are ranked

Search results are shown in this order: listings with an active boost — bought, or given free as a founding-creator perk — (always labelled "Sponsored"), then creators who usually reply quickly (within an hour, then within a few hours, then within a day — measured by our server from the last 90 days of messages, and only after at least 3 answered travellers), then all other listings; newest first within each group. City pages (capturago.com/in/…) show boosted listings first, then founding creators, then the same reply-time groups, then by rating. The trip planner also lists creators who take online bookings before those who don't. When you use AI match, an AI model ranks creators by how well their listing fits your description (location first, then service, style, budget and languages); between equally good fits it prefers faster replies. Paying for a boost never changes reviews, ratings or reply times.
