3. What is public

If you create a creator listing, everything in the listing except your contact email is visible to anyone visiting CapturaGo. Reviews are public together with the reviewer's display name. Inquiries are private — only the creator you message can see them.

5. Cookies and browser storage

CapturaGo uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. We use only strictly necessary browser storage, which does not require consent under EU law:

  • Sign-in session (local storage, key starting with sb-) — keeps you logged in. Removed when you sign out.
  • cg_notice_ok (local storage) — remembers that you closed the storage notice.

When your browser loads map images or destination photos, the provider (OpenStreetMap, Unsplash) receives your IP address, as with any website request. If we ever add analytics, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and listing data: until you delete your listing or ask us to delete your account.
  • Inquiries: until the creator's listing is deleted, or earlier if you ask us to delete them.
  • Booking and payment records: as long as accounting and tax law requires (in Slovakia generally 10 years), even if a listing or account is deleted.
  • Reviews: while the reviewed listing exists, unless you ask us to remove your review.
  • Server logs: according to our hosting providers' standard retention (usually days to weeks).

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data (creators can edit their listing anytime in the dashboard);
  • have your data deleted (creators can delete their listing in the dashboard; for full account deletion email us);
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time (e.g. location access in your browser settings);
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work.

To use any of these rights, email hello@capturago.com.

8. Age

CapturaGo is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to data in our database is restricted by row-level security rules so that users can only change their own data. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced on the website.
